Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 October 2026

Summary

The Drécibel Publisher is a private app used only by the channel owner to upload videos to the owner's own TikTok account. It does not collect data from other people, has no user accounts, no analytics, no cookies and no ads.

What data is processed

  • TikTok access data of the owner's own account: access token, refresh token and the account's open ID, received through TikTok Login Kit after the owner authorizes the app (scopes: user.info.basic, video.upload). Used only to upload the channel's own videos and to show the account name for confirmation.
  • The channel's own videos and captions, which are uploaded to the owner's TikTok account.

Where it is stored

Tokens are stored only locally on the owner's personal computer. They are never sent anywhere except to TikTok's official API, never shared with third parties and never published. This website is static: the callback page only displays the authorization code in the owner's own browser and does not transmit or store it.

Sharing

No data is sold or shared. Videos are sent only to TikTok, as requested by the owner.

Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept until the owner revokes access. Access can be revoked at any time in the TikTok app (Settings and privacy → Security → Apps and services); the local token file is then deleted.

Children

The app is not directed at children and does not process data of children.
